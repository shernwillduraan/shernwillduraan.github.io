Fair Value Gaps

Study imbalances created by displacement and learn how Fair Value Gaps can become areas of interest.

LESSON 1

Understanding Fair Value Gaps

Lesson Explanation

A Fair Value Gap is commonly identified as an imbalance across a three-candle sequence created by aggressive displacement. The gap can become an area of interest for a later retracement, but it is not an automatic entry.

Practical Exercise

Find bullish and bearish FVGs. Mark the gap boundaries and identify the displacement that created each one.

LESSON 2

Bullish & Bearish FVGs

Lesson Explanation

Bullish FVGs form during strong upward displacement and bearish FVGs during strong downward displacement. Always consider the surrounding structure, liquidity and location rather than trading every gap.

Practical Exercise

Mark five FVGs and classify them bullish or bearish. Record the surrounding structure and nearby liquidity.

LESSON 3

FVG Confluence

Lesson Explanation

An FVG becomes more useful when it aligns with a broader SMC narrative such as liquidity, order blocks, structure and premium or discount. The goal is to filter for quality rather than collect every imbalance.

Practical Exercise

Build one complete FVG setup: higher-timeframe location, liquidity event, displacement, FVG, entry idea and invalidation.
