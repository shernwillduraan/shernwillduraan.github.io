05 · SMC FRAMEWORK

Premium & Discount

Use a dealing range to understand whether price is trading in premium, equilibrium or discount.

LESSON 1

The Dealing Range

A dealing range is a defined high-to-low price range used to evaluate where current price sits. It should be anchored to meaningful structure rather than arbitrary points.

PRACTICAL EXERCISE

Select a clear higher-timeframe dealing range. Mark high, low and equilibrium and explain the boundaries.

LESSON 2

Premium, Equilibrium & Discount

The upper half of a dealing range is commonly called premium and the lower half discount, with equilibrium around the midpoint. These areas provide context, not automatic entries.

PRACTICAL EXERCISE

Find one bullish scenario in discount and one bearish scenario in premium. Identify supporting liquidity and structure.

LESSON 3

SMC Location & Confluence

Premium and discount become more useful when combined with liquidity, order blocks, FVGs and market structure. Location answers where to look; confirmation helps answer when to act.

PRACTICAL EXERCISE

Build one setup using premium or discount plus at least two other SMC concepts. Define entry, invalidation and target.
