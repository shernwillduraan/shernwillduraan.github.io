Premium & Discount

Use a dealing range to understand whether price is trading in premium, equilibrium or discount.

LESSON 1

The Dealing Range

Lesson Explanation

A dealing range is a meaningful high-to-low range used to evaluate where current price sits. Anchor the range to real structure rather than arbitrary points.

Practical Exercise

Select a clear higher-timeframe range. Mark the high, low and equilibrium and explain why those boundaries matter.

LESSON 2

Premium, Equilibrium & Discount

Lesson Explanation

The upper half of a dealing range is commonly called premium, the lower half discount and the midpoint equilibrium. These areas provide context; they do not replace structural confirmation.

Practical Exercise

Find one bullish scenario in discount and one bearish scenario in premium. Identify the supporting liquidity and structure.

LESSON 3

SMC Location & Confluence

Lesson Explanation

Premium and discount become more useful when combined with liquidity, order blocks, FVGs and structure. Location narrows the search; confirmation determines whether the setup is actionable.

Practical Exercise

Choose one premium or discount setup and document the full confluence before considering an entry.
