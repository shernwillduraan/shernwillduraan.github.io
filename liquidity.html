Liquidity

Understand where liquidity forms, why price may seek it, and how liquidity sweeps can become part of an SMC setup.

LESSON 1

Understanding Liquidity

Liquidity represents areas where orders are likely concentrated. Obvious highs and lows can attract attention because stops and pending orders may cluster around them. Treat liquidity as a location to study, not a promise.

PRACTICAL EXERCISE

Mark obvious highs and lows. Identify potential buy-side and sell-side liquidity.

LESSON 2

Buy-Side & Sell-Side Liquidity

Buy-side liquidity is commonly associated with stops above highs, while sell-side liquidity is commonly associated with stops below lows. Equal highs and equal lows can make these areas easier to identify.

PRACTICAL EXERCISE

Find two equal-high and two equal-low examples. Mark the liquidity pool and describe what happened when price approached it.

LESSON 3

Liquidity Sweeps & Reversals

A liquidity sweep occurs when price trades through an obvious liquidity area and then reacts. The sweep itself is not an automatic entry. Look for displacement, structural change and a valid entry area.

PRACTICAL EXERCISE

Study three sweeps. Identify the liquidity taken, reaction and structural confirmation.
