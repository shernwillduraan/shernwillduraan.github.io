Liquidity

Understand where liquidity forms, why price may seek it, and how liquidity sweeps can become part of an SMC setup.

LESSON 1

Understanding Liquidity

Lesson Explanation

Liquidity is studied as areas where orders and stops may be concentrated. Obvious highs, lows and equal levels can become important reference points. Treat liquidity as an area to analyse, not a promise that price must reach it.

Practical Exercise

Mark obvious highs and lows and identify which areas may represent buy-side and sell-side liquidity.

LESSON 2

Buy-Side & Sell-Side Liquidity

Lesson Explanation

Buy-side liquidity is commonly associated with stops above highs, while sell-side liquidity is commonly associated with stops below lows. Equal highs, equal lows and obvious swing points often make these pools easier to identify.

Practical Exercise

Find two examples of equal highs and two equal lows. Mark the liquidity pool and describe what price did around it.

LESSON 3

Liquidity Sweeps & Reversals

Lesson Explanation

A liquidity sweep occurs when price trades through an obvious liquidity area and then reacts. The sweep itself is not an entry. Study the reaction, displacement and structural confirmation that follows.

Practical Exercise

Study three sweeps. For each, identify the liquidity taken, the reaction and the confirmation that followed.
