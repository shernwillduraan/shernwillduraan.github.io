SMC concepts — foundations

These are general concept explanations, not a substitute for your personal entry rules or trading methodology.

Market structure

Price swings can form higher highs and higher lows in an uptrend, or lower highs and lower lows in a downtrend. A break of a meaningful swing may suggest a change in control, but context matters.

Liquidity

Liquidity is often discussed around obvious swing highs/lows and equal highs/lows where stop orders may cluster. A sweep alone does not guarantee reversal; wait for confirmation within a plan.

Order blocks

Traders commonly label a final opposing candle before a strong displacement as an order block. These zones are interpretive, can fail, and should not be treated as guaranteed institutional orders.

Fair value gaps

A fair value gap describes a three-candle price imbalance where the first and third candles leave an unoverlapped range. Price may revisit it, but it is not required to do so.

Break of structure

A break of structure (BOS) is commonly used for continuation when price breaks a relevant swing in the prevailing direction. The chosen swing and timeframe affect the reading.

Change of character

A change of character (CHOCH) is often used to describe an early break against the prior swing sequence. Treat it as a possible shift, not confirmation by itself.
