What You Will Learn

The Complete Academy Package is built around the core skills a developing trader needs to understand and practise.

Forex Foundations

Market basics, terminology, instruments, sessions and the foundations of forex trading.

Learn how to read swings, trends, breaks and important areas of price behaviour.
