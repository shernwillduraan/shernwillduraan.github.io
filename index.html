03 / Curriculum

What You Will Learn

The Complete Academy Package is built around the core skills a developing trader needs to understand and practise.

01

Forex Foundations

Market basics, terminology, instruments, sessions and the foundations of forex trading.

02

Market Structure

Learn how to read swings, trends, breaks and important areas of price behaviour.

Technical Analysis

Build a structured approach to charts, levels, confluence and market context.

04

Price Action

Study price behaviour and formations as part of a broader market analysis process.

Risk Management

Develop awareness around discipline, patience, emotions and decision-making.

Trade Execution

Live Market Analysis

Apply concepts to live charts and learn how analysis can be translated into a trading plan.
