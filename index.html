03 / Curriculum

What You'll Learn

Core subjects selected to give students a complete foundation for developing their own trading process.

Forex Foundations

Market basics, terminology, currency pairs, sessions and essential concepts.

Market Structure

Trends, swings, structural levels and ways of framing market direction.

Technical Analysis

Charts, levels, confluence and tools used to build market context.

Price Action

Reading price behaviour and formations as part of a broader analysis process.

Risk Management

Capital protection, position sizing, risk-to-reward concepts and trade limits.

Trading Psychology

Discipline, patience, emotional awareness and process-based decision making.

Trade Execution

Building entries, invalidation points, targets and a repeatable execution plan.

Live Market Analysis

Practical analysis sessions designed to connect concepts to market conditions.
