07 · LIVE APPLICATION

Live Market Analysis

Apply the complete SMC framework through a top-down process without forcing a trade when conditions are unclear.

LESSON 1

Top-Down SMC Analysis

Start from the higher timeframe and identify major structure, liquidity and important areas. Move through Daily, 4H and 1H only when the lower timeframe adds clarity.

PRACTICAL EXERCISE

Complete a Weekly → Daily → 4H → 1H analysis. Mark structure, liquidity, premium/discount and potential SMC areas.

LESSON 2

Scenario Planning & Confirmation

A market can develop in different ways. Build a primary and alternative scenario. Define what price must do to activate each and what would invalidate it.

PRACTICAL EXERCISE

Write two scenarios for the same instrument. Include liquidity objective, structural behaviour, entry area and invalidation.

LESSON 3

Complete SMC Market Model

Bring together higher-timeframe structure, liquidity, premium/discount, OB or FVG, displacement, confirmation, PSE execution and target. If conditions are absent, waiting can be the correct action.

PRACTICAL EXERCISE

Perform a complete analysis on a historical or live chart. Save your chart, thesis, entry model, invalidation and target, then review the outcome.
