LESSON 1

Top-Down SMC Analysis

Lesson Explanation

Start with the higher timeframe and work down only when the lower timeframe adds clarity. The Academy framework uses Weekly, Daily, 4H and then 1H when appropriate. Do not force a lower timeframe when the picture is unclear.

Practical Exercise

Perform a top-down analysis from Weekly to Daily to 4H. Write the bias, key liquidity and areas of interest.

LESSON 2

Scenario Planning & Confirmation

Lesson Explanation

Build a primary scenario and an alternative scenario. Each scenario needs an activation condition and an invalidation condition. This keeps analysis conditional instead of forcing a prediction.

Practical Exercise

Create two scenarios for the same market. Define exactly what price must do before either scenario becomes active.

GBPUSD · 1H · SCENARIO PLANNING & CONFIRMATIONAnnotated historical example · not live data
LESSON 3

Complete SMC Market Model

Lesson Explanation

A complete SMC model brings together higher-timeframe structure, liquidity, premium or discount, order blocks, FVGs, displacement, confirmation, PSE and targets. If the conditions are absent, waiting is part of the process.

Practical Exercise

Complete a full SMC market model and document the setup from position through target. If conditions are absent, record why you would wait.
