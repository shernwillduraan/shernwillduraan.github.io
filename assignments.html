PRACTICAL LEARNING

CHART ASSIGNMENTS.

Build evidence that you understand the lesson. Complete the chart task, explain your reasoning and check your work against the rubric. These assignments are practice activities, not financial advice or proof of future trading results.

For each task, use a historical chart or a demo environment. Never submit account passwords, private account details or sensitive personal information. This version records your work in this browser and does not upload screenshots or send submissions to a mentor.
01 · STRUCTURE

Map the market

Open a clean historical chart and mark the major swing highs and lows. Identify the current structural condition and one meaningful BOS or CHoCH, if present.

  • Mark swings and the relevant break.
  • State bullish, bearish or ranging context.
  • Explain what would invalidate your reading.
02 · LIQUIDITY

Study a liquidity event

Find an example of price reaching an obvious high or low. Record whether it swept the level and what happened afterwards.

  • Identify the likely liquidity pool.
  • Describe the reaction after the event.
  • Do not treat the sweep alone as an entry signal.
03 · CONFLUENCE

Filter an SMC setup

Choose a historical example involving at least two concepts, such as structure, liquidity, an order block, an FVG or premium/discount.

  • Describe the location and market context.
  • Define a possible entry trigger and invalidation.
  • Explain why the setup could fail.
04 · RISK & REVIEW

Plan and review a demo trade

Use a demo or backtesting environment. Document the planned risk, stop, target and the rule you will review after the result.

  • Set the invalidation before entry.
  • Calculate risk before simulating the trade.
  • Record whether you followed your rules.
