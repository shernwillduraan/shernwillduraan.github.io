Learn how Smart Money Concepts begins with understanding the structure of price and the sequence of highs and lows.

LESSON 1

Understanding Market Structure

Lesson Explanation

Market structure is the foundation of SMC. Read the sequence of meaningful highs and lows first, then decide whether price is showing bullish, bearish or ranging behaviour. Do not predict the next move before identifying what price has already confirmed.

Practical Exercise

LESSON 2

Break of Structure (BOS)

Lesson Explanation

A BOS occurs when price breaks a meaningful structural point in the direction of the current move. Use the break as evidence of continuation, not as an automatic entry. The quality of a BOS depends on the structure and timeframe being broken.

Practical Exercise

Find three bullish and three bearish BOS examples. Mark the broken structural level and explain why it was meaningful.

LESSON 3

CHoCH & Structural Shifts

Lesson Explanation

CHoCH describes a meaningful change in price behaviour that can warn that the previous structure is losing control. It is context, not a guaranteed reversal. Look for confirmation through liquidity, displacement and a valid area.

Practical Exercise

Find two structural shifts. Mark the shift and write what additional confirmation you would require before entry.
