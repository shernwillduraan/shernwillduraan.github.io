Market Structure

Learn how Smart Money Concepts begins with understanding the structure of price and the sequence of highs and lows.

LESSON 1

Understanding Market Structure

SMC CHART STUDYSWING STRUCTURE
Educational schematic using candlestick-style price action — not live market data.

Market structure is the foundation of SMC. Price moves through sequences of highs and lows. A bullish structure generally prints higher highs and higher lows, while bearish structure generally prints lower lows and lower highs. Your first job is to read what price has actually done before predicting what it may do next.

PRACTICAL EXERCISE

Mark the major swing highs and swing lows on a clean chart. Label the current structure bullish, bearish or ranging and explain why.

LESSON 2

Break of Structure (BOS)

SMC CHART STUDYBOS CONFIRMATION
Educational schematic using candlestick-style price action — not live market data.

A Break of Structure occurs when price breaks a meaningful structural point in the direction of the current move. BOS is evidence of continuation, not a blind entry signal. The importance of the break depends on timeframe and structure.

PRACTICAL EXERCISE

Find three bullish and three bearish BOS examples. Identify the structural level that was broken.

LESSON 3

CHoCH & Structural Shifts

SMC CHART STUDYCHoCH / SHIFT
Educational schematic using candlestick-style price action — not live market data.

A Change of Character describes a meaningful change in price behaviour that can warn that the previous structure is losing control. CHoCH is context, not a guaranteed reversal. Look for liquidity, displacement and confirmation.

PRACTICAL EXERCISE

Find two structural shifts and explain what additional evidence would be needed before considering an entry.
